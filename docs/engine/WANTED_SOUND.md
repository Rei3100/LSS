# Engine | Wanted Sound (STEM target)

The screen calls this target **STEM** (internal identifier `SOUND`, kept so saved state stays readable). The user picks stems from chips (ボーカル, コーラス, ドラム, ベース, ギター, ピアノ, ストリングス, シンセ, 管楽器; more under その他) and combinations (`ベース+ギター`, `コーラス+伴奏`); one run saves every single stem and every combination listed. There is no free-text field on the screen; the word grammar below is what the chips send (a combination is sent as `AとB`).

The request is a list of wanted sounds; LSS chooses the route and publishes `<source> - <label>.flac` beside the source (`<source folder>\LSS`). This is the third semantic target next to INSTRUMENTAL and BGM. Code: `lss/sound/`; benchmarks and rejected routes: `release-evidence/wanted-sound/EVIDENCE.md`; worker job `cmd=wanted_sound` (IPC protocol 8).

**Status:** resumed as STEM (2026-11-05): stem delivery for the work's music, with dedicated separation models wherever one exists. Text-queried separation stays only as the fallback for instruments no model outputs.

## Meaning of the words
- Japanese and English words resolve through a curated lexicon (`lss/sound/lexicon.py`, ~280 concepts, ~1,600 words): speech and voices, lead/backing/all vocals, whole music, instrumental, effects, instruments and sections (incl. Japanese and world instruments), human sounds, animals, vehicles, household, tools, weapons, materials, nature, and common onomatopoeia (`ドカーン`, `ザーザー`, `ピーポー`). Each concept carries an English noun phrase for text-queried separation and the AudioSet classes used to verify it.
- `ボーカル` is the **lead vocal only** (no chorus, no harmony). `コーラス` / `ハーモニー` resolve to the backing vocals (everything sung except the lead), published as `コーラス・ハーモニー`; chorus and harmony are not separated from each other (see Limits). `歌声全部` is all vocals.
- Composition: `AとB` / `A + B` / `A・B` / `A and B` (one asset), `Aなし` / `A抜き` / `Aを消して` / `without A` (the remainder), `BGMのA` / `A in the music` (scoped), `ドラムのシンバル` (a part of a stem instrument). Several wanted sounds are separated by `、` `，` `,` `;` `；` `/` `／` `|` or new lines (`_SEPARATORS`); each becomes its own asset. Union joiners (`_UNION`): `と` `＆` `&` `+` `＋` `・` ` and ` ` with `.
- **Narrowed sounds**: a lexicon modifier (`lexicon.MODIFIERS`: who/what/where/how, e.g. `子ども`, `怪獣`, `遠く`, `お寺`, `激しい`) before a text-separated lexicon sound keeps that sound's route and verification and narrows only the words (`子どもの笑い声` → laughter route, prompt `child laughter`; `男性の低い歌声` → male singing, `low male singing`). A modifier on a stem-only sound (drums, bass) reads as the stem. An English phrase that names exactly one lexicon sound is narrowed the same way (`distant thunder`, `sci-fi laser shots`).
- **Measured parents**: open phrases and lexicon sounds that can live in dialogue, music or effects (`口笛`, `鐘`, `拍手`, `オルゴール`, `銅鑼`, `木魚`, `お経` …, `parent="auto"`) are extracted from the candidate parent where PE-A-Frame hears the words most; without any evidence a lexicon sound keeps its family's parent.
- **Japanese outside the lexicon** (`lss/sound/interpret.py`) is translated (opus-mt-ja-en n-best) and a candidate is used only when it is **grounded**: the lexicon sounds named in the English must all be named in the Japanese (`雷が落ちる音` → "thunder falling" is kept; `猫が窓を引っかく音` → "a dog scratching" is dropped). When every top candidate names the same single sound of the Japanese but the details disagree, that sound is read (`agreed`). Japanese that names no lexicon sound needs both multilingual judges (LaBSE ≥ 0.70 and multilingual MiniLM ≥ 0.90 for an open phrase). Free-standing single-kanji words count only after a particle or an inflection ending (`かめはめ波` names no wave). What was searched is reported as `heard_as`.
- **Time ranges**: `前半の` / `後半の`, `最初の30秒の` / `最後の10秒の`, `1分から2分の` / `1分30秒から2分までの`, `30秒からの`, `2分までの`, `first 30 seconds of …`, `… from 0:10 to 0:40`. The sound is extracted over the whole source (nodes are shared with unranged requests), then everything outside the range is silenced with 50 ms fades before presence is decided, so presence means presence *within the range*. The range is part of the request identity and of the file name (`<source> - 前半の足音.flac`).
- Nothing else is guessed: unread words are `unsupported` with the words and up to three close lexicon words as hints (`ギタ` → `ギター`); place (left/right) and loudness scopes and vague times (`序盤`) are `scope_not_supported`.

## Routes (`lss/sound/plan.py`)
Nodes: `source`, `dnr` (SFC-Locoformer speech/music/effects, the BGM analyzer), `vocals` (becruily deux, the INSTRUMENTAL default), `lead` (becruily BS-RoFormer karaoke on the all-vocals stem), `stems` (SCNet XL IHF: drums/bass/other/vocals; the parent for text fallbacks), `model` (a dedicated model's output; every output of one model and parent is computed in one inference, `extract.MODELS`), `text` (SAM Audio base-tv, mask-projected onto its parent), `sum`, `subtract`.
- A source is `cinematic` when PANNs hears speech (P ≥ 0.5) in ≥ 8 % of seconds; then the music parent is `dnr.music` and the effects parent `dnr.effects`. Otherwise (a song) the parent is the source itself and `音楽` is a no-op representation.
- Speech, music, effects → `dnr`. Lead vocal → `lead(vocals(music))`; backing → `vocals − lead`; all vocals → `vocals(music)`; instrumental → `vocals(music).instrumental`. This `伴奏` is the plain separator output on the music parent: the INSTRUMENTAL target additionally runs selective original composition (`lss/separation/selective.py`, a `selective_original_composition` stage in its separation Recipe), which the SOUND route does not; the two files can differ for the same source.
- Male / female singing → `voices(vocals(music))`: the chorus male/female BS-RoFormer (`Sucial/Chorus_Male_Female_BS_Roformer@bb174dc`, ep 267, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, experimental, trained on Chinese songs) instead of text separation. Modifiers do not change this stem.
- **Dedicated models first** (`plan.specialist`). Drums, bass, guitar, piano → BS-RoFormer SW 6stem on music (one inference, so `ベース+ギター` is a sum of outputs of the same inference and has no doubled or missing energy). Kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, ride, crash → DrumSep MDX23C 6stem on the SW6 drums (`シンバル` = ride + crash). Strings → gilliaan BowedStrings v2. Acoustic/electric guitar, organ, synth, brass, woodwind, winds, violin, viola, cello, harp, flute, trumpet, trombone, horn, tuba, clarinet, oboe, bassoon, saxophone, harpsichord, keys, electric piano, double bass, timpani, tambourine, triangle, congas, percussion, accordion, harmonica, banjo, mandolin, ukulele, sitar, glockenspiel → MVSep Mega 53stem (full checkpoint, one inference). Weights are the public files whose SHA-256 equals the development copies (`noblebarkrr/mvsepless_resources@2830252`, `gilliaan/Stem-Separation-Models@62fe7d8`), fetched on first use; DrumSep runs with the development inference overlap (`num_overlap: 4`).
- Fallback only (no model outputs the instrument): other drum parts (taiko, tsuzumi, bass drum, maracas, cowbell, castanets, drum roll, tabla) → `text` on `stems.drums`; synth bass → `text` on `stems.bass`; choir → `text` on music; every other instrument → `text` on `stems.other` in **instrument mode** (below).
- **Instrument mode** (`text` with `instrument`): PANNs tags on `stems.other` mark the seconds where only the asked instrument plays (asked classes ≥ 0.2, every other melodic/harmonic instrument class < half of it; related concepts such as piano/electric piano are not rivals); there the parent is kept whole. Elsewhere the SAM mask is floored at 0.5, because text separation drops parts of an instrument per frequency (`stems.other` alone 8.6 dB, plain SAM 0.5 dB on real songs).
- Specific effects and ambience → `text` on the effects parent; open English phrases → `text` on the source.

## Text-queried separation, faithfully (`lss/sound/sam_audio.py`)
SAM Audio generates 48 kHz mono codec audio for the target and the residual. LSS never publishes that waveform. It builds a power-ratio mask `|T|²/(|T|²+|R|²)` (STFT 2048/512, Hann) at the source rate and applies it to each original channel of the parent. The published sound is a subset of the recorded mixture: stereo, original rate and bandwidth, no hallucinated content, no codec watermark. Chunks of 20 s with 2 s linear crossfades; fixed noise seed; midpoint ODE, 16 steps; DiT under bfloat16 autocast; codec and T5 in float32.
- Upstream code is vendored byte-identical (`vendor/sam_audio`, verified against the upstream git blobs); the thin `SAMAudio` text path and the T5-base encoder/tokenizer are reimplemented with the same parameter names and loaded strictly. T5 parity with `transformers`: 0/449 tokenizer mismatches, max encoder abs diff 0.0.
- Weights: `AEmotionStudio/sam-audio-models@5ee10e3` `sam-audio-base-tv-bf16.safetensors` (SAM License redistribution, anonymous). All 1237 tensors equal `facebook/sam-audio-base-tv@ab0acdd` `checkpoint.pt` cast to bfloat16. Vision-encoder tensors are not loaded.

## Verification (`lss/sound/verify.py`)

Brief class-backed effects can also be accepted for extraction when three consecutive 40 ms frames have word probability at least 0.8 in both the recorded parent and the output, inside audible output seconds with the unchanged 0.03 requested-class threshold. This corroboration does not lower the existing thresholds, apply to open words, instruments or speech, or authorize removal; it is a bounded recall improvement, not a general short-sound guarantee.
Short named-effect removal adds a bounded confirmation path when the two-second tag test misses a short effect: at least ten coincident 40 ms word frames must reach 0.8 in both the extracted output and its recorded parent, inside audible seconds with target AudioSet evidence at least 0.03 and unchanged dominance over unrelated named effects. Only positively tagged dominant seconds are removed (normal 50 ms fades); zero-versus-zero rival ties do not extend support; instruments, singing/speech and open phrases get no new permission. This is coarse tag corroboration, not 40 ms tag localization. (The code is contained in the deployed 6c93897; its numbers are Evidence, not contract.)
Separators return something even when the target is absent (asked for "knocking" on a clapping stem, SAM returned the whole stem). Every output is tagged per second with PANNs CNN14. The wanted sound is present when its classes reach 0.03 in ≥ 2 audible seconds; speech evidence does not count where singing classes are stronger and vice versa; speech must be within 25 dB of the source. Sparse sounds (specific effects, voices) keep only seconds with evidence ≥ 0.015 (±1 s, 50 ms fades). Absence is recorded as a validated zero result (`no_wanted_target`) and is reused.
- **Instruments** are decided on the parent: the instrument-mode output is a part of `stems.other` (floored mask, whole seconds), so presence is the PE-A-Frame word evidence of the asked instrument *in the parent* (≥ 10 frames ≥ 0.8; `decide(parent_presence=True)`). Deciding on the output called absent instruments present 19/52 (asking `ギター` on a piano song: 6/6) and published 36/42 asked instruments; on the parent: 6/52 and 39/42. A PANNs contrast between instrument classes as an extra gate lost true instruments (31/42) and is not used.

### Removal requests

`Aなし` verifies each removed sound before subtraction. A present atom cannot authorize removing another atom. Text-separated targets need independent AudioSet output confirmation, PE word evidence in the output and parent, and (specific effects) requested-class evidence at least as strong as unrelated named effect classes. AudioSet ontology ancestors/descendants are excluded from competition (`audioset_relations.py`, Google AudioSet ontology, CC BY-SA 4.0). Only those supported times are removed, with the normal short fades. Instrument retention floors are never used to authorize subtraction. Conflicting evidence, unsupported independent verification, and overlapping text-removal estimates are HELD rather than blindly subtracted.

When the target is not confirmed in either output or parent, removal is a no-op: save the source through normal serialization, explicitly explain that it was retained, and reuse that result without inference. This is not a zero wanted-result. `前半のAなし` removes only in the first half; the remainder keeps the full source clock and samples outside the requested window. PCM equality is subject to the normal input/output serialization contract (float inputs can be scaled), not a new lossless-float claim. Removal provenance and its unchanged/removed disposition survive reuse in `LSS_SCORE`.

## Falsifiers kept (details and numbers: `release-evidence/wanted-sound/EVIDENCE.md`)
- Absent sound asked for must be reported absent, not published (SAM returns something regardless); a present atom never authorizes removing another; instruments are decided on the parent, not the output; singing is never recovered from the dialogue stem on a PE gate; no codec-generated waveform is ever published; harmony/chorus split by signal processing is not shipped.

## Limits
- Verification recall for specific effects is ~70 % on the calibration set: a present sound can be reported as not found. Classes PANNs does not know well (laser, magic, robot) rely on generic classes.
- Chorus versus harmony is not separated. Instruments without a dedicated model use text separation inside SCNet "other" (instrument mode); when another instrument plays at the same time the result stays below doing nothing (−3.3 vs −1.8 dB), and about one ask in nine for an absent instrument is still called present (6/52). The dedicated-model routes have published multisong figures (SW6 guitar 9.05, piano 7.83 dB) but no LSS bench row yet on anime BGM.
- Long sources: SAM costs ~0.2 RTF per wanted sound on the RTX 5060 Ti; weights are fetched on first use (3.86 GB SAM + 0.89 GB T5 + 0.21 GB SCNet + 0.20 GB karaoke + 0.53 GB male/female + 2.54 GB PE-A-Frame; reading Japanese outside the lexicon adds 0.30 GB opus-mt and 1.88 / 0.47 GB judges).
- Male/female singing: voices that alternate are split poorly by every route tried (specialist ~0–2 dB); a solo singer can be partly assigned to the other voice.
- Outputs are time-aligned full-length stems; silence where the sound is absent.
