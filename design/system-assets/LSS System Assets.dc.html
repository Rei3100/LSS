A CORE VISUAL PRINCIPLE
Three rules, and nothing else, carry LSS at system scale. Everything in the main UI that is not on this list is deliberately left out of these assets.
1 · THE LEAN
Every vertical edge leans −14°, the same shear the main UI uses on bars and badges. Nothing in this system is upright, and nothing is rounded. Corners stay sharp at every size.
2 · ONE CUT
A single straight cut at 15° from horizontal crosses the block. One cut only — a second cut halves the mass of each plane and the shape stops reading below 32 px.
3 · DISPLACEMENT
The two planes slide apart along the cut, leaving a kerf of open ground. The upper plane is red, the lower is bone: two-tone, so the mark still separates in grayscale and at low attention.
LEFT OUT ON PURPOSE
per-row rotation · dot texture · oversized numerals · bilingual pairs · gradients · blur · hard shadow below 48 px

The shadow and the cut angle turn into grey fringing at 16 px. The redrawn version keeps only the two bars, the lean, and a 2 px kerf, snapped to whole pixels.
GROUND TESTS
On white, the bone plane needs the 2 u black backing plane to keep its edge. On red ground, the upper plane inverts to ink. Grayscale still separates, because value — not hue — carries the split.
C SYSTEM TRAY ICON
Mostly monochrome with one accent plane. The ink plane follows the taskbar theme; the accent plane carries state. State is also carried by geometry — kerf width, lateral offset, lean direction — so it survives grayscale, peripheral vision and colour-blind perception.

MOTION · RESTRAINED
No continuous animation. PROCESSING alternates the lateral offset between 3 and 4 px on a 1.2 s step — two frames, no easing, readable as a slow pulse and invisible as a distraction. COMPLETE plays the offset opening to kerf 4 once, then holds.
STATES NOT MADE
No per-stage icons (PREPARE / SEPARATE / SAVE) and no progress ring in the tray: at 16 px they are indistinguishable from PROCESSING, and progress belongs in the menu where there is room for digits.
D CURSOR FAMILY

CONTRAST TESTS · OVER REAL LSS SURFACES
Over saturated LSS surfaces (red slab, cyan running row, gold saved badge) the backing plane switches to ink. Bone body plus ink edge is the invariant; the plane's colour is not load-bearing.
NOT CUSTOMISED
Text I-beam, resize arrows, and no-drop keep Windows defaults. They carry no LSS meaning, they are the cursors users scrutinise most for precision, and a styled I-beam makes caret placement ambiguous. Restraint here is what keeps the four custom cursors legible as a family.
